My strategy for trustworthy enterprise AI

Four layers I apply to any system where a language model sits near money, customers or decisions. Pick a layer to see how I built it and where it applies in a business.

How I built it

Facts come from code, never from the model.

Planetary positions are calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris and legacy interpretation rules are encoded in Python. Gemini only receives results that have already been computed.

Where it applies

Systems of record decide, AI explains.

Balances, eligibility, pricing and claims values should come from the governed system, with the model summarising them for people.

How I built it

One controlled handoff between engine and model.

The Interpreter only ever sees a structured Fact Pack of verified results. The orchestrator also discovers live Gemini models, ranks them, and fails over with exponential jitter backoff on 404 and 429 errors.

Where it applies

An AI gateway, not a free-for-all.

Routing, fallbacks and caching sit in one place, so cost, resilience and data flow can be reviewed and changed without touching every feature.

How I built it

Prompt rules that limit what the model may say.

A zero-token Critic checks the model’s output for invented dates and Dasa periods, and failures trigger a revision capped at two retries. “Natal First” rules and cancellation logic such as Kemadruma prevent false positives.

Where it applies

Policy as part of the design.

Tone, scope and escalation rules belong in the specification and the prompt, then get tested like any other business rule.

How I built it

Prove it, then keep proving it.

I audited the Python rules and corrected critical calculation defects, ran functional and regression tests, verified the English and Tamil workflows, and simulated HTTP 404, 429 and 503 failures.

Where it applies

Evidence a risk team can sign.

Traceable test cases, failure-mode results and regression suites turn “the AI seems fine” into a release decision.

Case study: Vidhu Astro Prediction

An AI-assisted Vedic astrology web application, built with Python, Flask and Google Gemini.

The business problem
Traditional astrology reports are static and open to ambiguous interpretation, and a language model on its own invents planetary degrees, date spans and Dasa calculations.
The transformation
Legacy rules became code in a two-layer system. pyswisseph supplies exact degrees, house cusps and Vimshottari Dasa timelines, and a Researcher, Interpreter and Critic pipeline turns them into a grounded narrative in English or Tamil.
My role
Requirements, workflow design, REST API and Gemini integration, debugging, error handling, and functional and regression testing.
Stack
Python 3.13, Flask, SQLite in WAL mode, pyswisseph, Google GenAI SDK, n8n automation, Jinja2 and print-ready CSS.

Analytics that turn data into decisions

Two projects from my Executive MBA in Business Analytics and Digital Transformation at PES University, both built in KNIME.

AI-driven customer segmentation for B2C online retail

Individual dissertation

RFM, customer lifetime value and K-Means clustering on the UCI Online Retail II data produced five segments, from Champions to Lost Customers, each with a recommended marketing action.

A 55-response survey showed recommendation accuracy and offer usefulness significantly predict engagement and purchase intention. A Tableau dashboard ties it together.

Code and report

Optimizing campaign timing with real-time analytics

Team project, six members

EDA, ANOVA and regression on about 200,000 campaign records from five companies. The ROI model reached R² 0.89, with acquisition cost and profit as the strongest drivers.

Time pattern and frequency came close to statistical significance, and the morning slot carried about half of total profit.
