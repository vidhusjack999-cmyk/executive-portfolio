Case study: Vidhu Astro Prediction

An AI-assisted Vedic astrology web application, built with Python, Flask and Google Gemini.

The business problem
Traditional astrology reports are static and open to ambiguous interpretation, and a language model on its own invents planetary degrees, date spans and Dasa calculations.
The transformation
Legacy rules became code in a two-layer system. pyswisseph supplies exact degrees, house cusps and Vimshottari Dasa timelines, and a Researcher, Interpreter and Critic pipeline turns them into a grounded narrative in English or Tamil.
My role
Requirements, workflow design, REST API and Gemini integration, debugging, error handling, and functional and regression testing.
Level 4 hybrid architectureClassical engine plus LLM, with a strict handoff between them
Zero-token CriticPython checks catch invented dates and Dasa periods, with up to 2 retries
Resilient by designLive model discovery, jitter backoff on 404 and 429, SQLite WAL quota tracking
English and TamilBilingual interface with location lookup and print-ready reports
PDF in, grounded answer outUploaded horoscopes combined with structured prompts
