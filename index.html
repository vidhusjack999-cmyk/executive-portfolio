EXECUTIVE MBA · FINAL PRESENTATION

AI-Driven Customer Segmentation & Marketing Effectiveness

Dissertation presentation submitted to PES University under the guidance of Akram Ali. Integrates secondary transactional data with primary survey perceptions.

📊 Research Objectives & Methodology

  • Dual Approach: Combines primary survey responses (55 online shoppers via Google Forms) with secondary transactional behavior (UCI Online Retail II dataset).
  • Tools Used: KNIME Analytics Platform (data modeling/segmentation), Excel (data cleaning), and Tableau (dashboards).
  • Core Metrics: RFM Analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary), Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), and K-Means clustering[cite: 12].

📈 Regression Analysis & Hypothesis Testing

  • H1 & H2 Supported (p < 0.05): AI-driven segmentation and personalized marketing positively influence customer engagement ($R^2 = 0.5861$) and purchase intention ($R^2 = 0.3943$)[cite: 12].
  • Key Drivers: Recommendation accuracy ($\beta = 0.5271$) and usefulness of personalized offers ($\beta = 0.3039$) are significant positive predictors[cite: 12].
  • Reliability: High internal consistency confirmed with a Cronbach’s alpha of 0.949[cite: 12].

🎯 K-Means Customer Segments & Targeted Business Actions

1. Champions Recent, frequent, high-value buyers[cite: 12]. Action: Retain & reward with exclusive loyalty benefits[cite: 12].
2. Loyal Customers Strong spend and frequency[cite: 12]. Action: Grow value through upselling and cross-selling[cite: 12].
3. Potential Loyalists Moderate activity[cite: 12]. Action: Nurture with targeted product recommendations[cite: 12].
4. At Risk Declining activity[cite: 12]. Action: Win back via reminders and targeted incentives[cite: 12].
5. Lost Customers Low recent activity[cite: 12]. Action: Selective and cost-effective re-engagement campaigns[cite: 12].
