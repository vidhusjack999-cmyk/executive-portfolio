My strategy for trustworthy enterprise AI

Four core layers I apply whenever language models sit near critical data, financial decisions, or customer workflows.

How I built it

Facts come from code, never from the model.

Planetary positions are calculated via Swiss Ephemeris C-bindings (`pyswisseph`) and classical rules are strictly encoded in Python. Gemini only receives results that have already been computed.

Where it applies

Systems of record decide, AI summarizes.

Balances, eligibility criteria, pricing calculations, and policy compliance values should come from governed backend systems, with the model acting purely as an interface.

How I built it
